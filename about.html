Our story

About DinoWorld

Making the fascinating history of dinosaurs accessible, visual and exciting for curious minds.

Our mission

Bringing prehistoric life closer

DinoWorld is an educational student website about dinosaurs and the world they lived in. We organize information about species, geological periods and fossils in one easy-to-explore place.

Our goal is to make learning enjoyable for students, families and anyone interested in natural history.
