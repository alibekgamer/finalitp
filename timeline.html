Geological Timeline

Discover the three major periods of the Mesozoic Era, the age of dinosaurs.

252–201 million years ago

Triassic Period

The earliest dinosaurs appeared and began to diversify on the supercontinent Pangaea.

201–145 million years ago

Jurassic Period

Giant sauropods and other dinosaurs flourished as the continents began to separate.

145–66 million years ago

Cretaceous Period

Many famous dinosaur groups evolved before the mass extinction at the end of the period.
